AI National Summit 3.0 – Workshop Alert! 💜
Join us for an exclusive workshop "Introduction to the transformer architecture" with Achref Ben Ammar! 
📅 Tuesday, April 8
⏰ 7 PM
📍 Google Meet
In this session, we’ll break down the core concepts behind the Transformer architecture — the foundation of today’s leading LLMs like Claude, GPT, and BERT. We’ll explore the basics of sequence models and how Transformers revolutionized them using attention and self-attention, enabling the capture of complex patterns in text without recurrence. Now the go-to architecture in NLP, Transformers have transformed how machines understand language.
